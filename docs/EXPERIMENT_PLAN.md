# Experiment plan — do not activate before baseline

The TZ explicitly places A/B testing after sufficient traffic.

## Baseline first
Minimum: 30-day stable measurement period or enough qualified lead volume to avoid optimizing noise.

## Test candidates
1. Hero proof:
   - A: founder credential
   - B: contextual case proof
2. Form order:
   - A: company → maturity → contact
   - B: company → task → maturity/contact
3. Case recommendation:
   - A: default case grid
   - B: segment-led recommended case

## Primary metric
Qualified lead rate.

## Guardrails
Form completion, sales acceptance rate, diagnostic sale rate, page speed. Do not call a winner on CTA rate alone.
